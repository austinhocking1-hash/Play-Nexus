Nexus Flight

A pseudo-3D flight sim. Fly through the gold rings over mountains and sea, watch your fuel, then land on the runway for a bonus. Stall, crash or run dry and it is over.

Score: 0 Mission: 1 Rings: 0 Best: 0

Controls: Arrows steer (Up = nose up, Left/Right = bank). THROTTLE: W / Shift = more, S / Ctrl = less, or tap the + and − buttons and drag the bar on the right of the screen. The plane levels itself when you let go. Land slow and level.
