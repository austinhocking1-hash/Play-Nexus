Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This explains what Play Nexus ("we") collects and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account details: username, email address and a securely hashed password (we can't read your password).
  • Gameplay data: scores, NexBucks, streaks, badges, who you follow, and notifications.
  • Suggestions and bug reports you submit, plus an optional name.
  • Technical data: your IP address may be used briefly to limit abuse, and game pages send error reports when something breaks.
  • A session cookie keeps you signed in. We also store small bits of data in your browser (like your best scores and a random anonymous ID used to count unique visitors).

How it's used

To run your account, show leaderboards and profiles (your username, badges and scores are public), award prizes, fix bugs and prevent cheating. We don't sell your personal information.

Email

Your email is not shown publicly. We only use it for your account.

Ads and third parties

If ads are shown, ad providers such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads. You can manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings. Hosting providers (Netlify, Render) process requests to deliver the Site.

Your choices

You can stop using the Site any time. To ask for your account or data to be deleted, contact us through the Suggestions page. Because data may be reset on our free hosting, we can't guarantee how long it is kept.

Children

Play Nexus is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. See also our Terms of Service.
